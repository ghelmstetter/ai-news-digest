Now to Google, which announced two specialized versions of its custom A.I. chips arriving next year. The T.P.U. eight T.X. is built for secure and confidential inference: data stays encrypted even while an A.I. model is working on it, a step where it normally sits exposed. Governments and regulated firms are the targets. The T.P.U. eight I Max joins two chips as one unit to double processing power and memory speed, aimed at massive context windows, for agents that need to keep an entire codebase or a long video in view.

Google began selling T.P.U.s to other companies this year and has nearly one hundred customers, including Anthropic and Apple, with every available chip spoken for. Amin Vahdat, who runs Google's chip work, told Bloomberg, "The future is going to be about specialization." The Rundown notes the timing, coming the day after Nvidia C.E.O.
