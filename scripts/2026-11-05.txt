Before we wrap, two practical notes from The Rundown's training section. First, a tip from Rundown educator Nate Grahek on getting more out of a twenty-dollar A.I. plan. His point is that every session is finite, and most people burn their credits making huge changes to a deck or website that's already been built. His advice: before anything gets built, tell the A.I. not to start yet.
